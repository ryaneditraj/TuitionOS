# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Trinity One to be a welcoming project for students, teachers, and contributors. We pledge to make participation free from harassment for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that helps the community:

- Showing empathy and assuming good intent
- Giving and receiving feedback kindly
- Focusing on what is best for the students and teachers who use the app
- Taking responsibility when a mistake affects someone else

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This code applies in the repository, issue tracker, pull requests, and any other space where someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainer, Ryan Editraj, through a private GitHub contact at https://github.com/ryaneditraj or by opening a GitHub security advisory if the report itself is sensitive. The maintainer will review the report and may remove content, lock a conversation, or block a participant.
